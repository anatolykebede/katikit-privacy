Privacy Policy for KaTi Kit

Effective date: October 28, 2026

This policy describes how the KaTi Kit Android app handles information. KaTi Kit is a reader for tips, guides, and how-tos. It does not ask you to create an account.

Information stored on your device

If you save a post, the app stores that post’s identifier on your device only. Those favorites are not uploaded to us. Uninstalling the app removes them. Text size while you read a post is kept only for that visit and is not saved.

Articles loaded from our server

The app downloads articles from tipsapp-server.praxisnest.com over HTTPS. That request includes your IP address so the server can send the article. We do not ask for your name, email address, or phone number, and we do not use these requests to create an account or a profile of you.

Advertising

The Android app shows ads through Google AdMob. Reading a post does not require you to watch an ad. You can choose to watch a short video to hide ads for 30 minutes. That choice is kept in the app’s memory and is lost if the app is closed.

Google’s Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares the following with Google for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention:

  • Device or other identifiers, including the Android advertising ID
  • Approximate location, estimated from the IP address
  • App interactions, such as app launches, taps, and views of ads
  • Diagnostic information, such as app launch time, hang rate, and energy use

The app does not request access to your precise GPS location. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. Google’s description of this SDK is at developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/play-data-disclosure.

Sharing

We do not sell your information. The advertising data above is shared with Google so the app can show ads. We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

Children

KaTi Kit is not directed at children. It is meant for people 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

Article requests and ad requests are sent over encrypted connections. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Deletion

Favorites stay on your device. Uninstall the app to remove them. We do not hold an account for you that we can delete. Advertising data held by Google is handled under Google’s policies. You can contact us with a question about this app’s data practices.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
